Privacy policy

Prompt Anatomy is a minimal app. Here’s a brief note about your data.

Do we collect your data on the free library?

No. The interactive prompt library has no form, email collection, or server-side submission.

What happens on your device?

Only your browser’s localStorage: we save which prompts you marked as “Done.” This data stays on your device.

If you buy a paid PDF (Starter or Pro)

When you check out one of the paid PDFs sold on promptanatomy.space, the following processors are involved. Each is used only to deliver the product you bought, not for marketing:

  • Stripe (USA) - processes the payment and stores your card details, billing email and country. We do not see or store full card numbers. Stripe privacy policy.
  • Resend (USA) - sends the receipt and download-link email from info@promptanatomy.app. Resend privacy policy.
  • Upstash Redis (USA, EU) - short-term store for the fulfillment lock and the signed download-token metadata (auto-expired within ~24 hours). Upstash privacy.
  • Vercel Blob (USA) - private storage for the PDF file. The file is served only via short-lived signed URLs to paying buyers. Vercel privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosting and Web Analytics (USA, anonymized) - serves promptanatomy.space and aggregates anonymous page-view data without tracking individuals across sites.

For each successful purchase we keep the minimum needed to deliver the product and honor refunds: your email, the Stripe checkout session id, the product purchased (Starter or Pro), and the timestamp. We do not add you to a marketing list. To request deletion, email us from the address you used to buy and we will remove our copy within 7 days; Stripe retains transaction records as required by US tax and anti-fraud law. Full team license and 14-day refund policy.

If we add a form later

If we enable a contact form or marketing list, we’ll update this policy and clearly state what we collect and how we use it.
